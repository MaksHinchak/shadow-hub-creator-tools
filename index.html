Private channel management tool

Creator workflow for Shadow Hub

This locally operated desktop integration helps the owner of the Shadow Hub channel on YouTube prepare and upload videos, manage video details and comments, and review channel analytics through Google-authorized APIs.

How access works

The owner authorizes access with Google OAuth. This is an internal tool for one channel, not a public service or a way for visitors to connect their own channels. The integration runs on the owner's computer using the open-source youtube-mcp-server package.

Privacy and control

Google credentials are kept on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account and delete the local credential file. Read the Privacy Policy for the data used, storage, sharing, and deletion details.

Questions about this operator's integration can be sent through the Shadow Hub Discord.
